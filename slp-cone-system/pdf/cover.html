Training Cone System

System Architecture & Project Plan

A swarm of self‑driving traffic cones for driver training. The instructor draws or selects an exercise on a tablet; the cones assign themselves to positions and drive there together without colliding. Selecting a different exercise rearranges the pad in under a minute.
